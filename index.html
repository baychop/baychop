Guidance only. The decision is yours.

This is a statistical forecast, not an official product, and it will sometimes be wrong by more than the ranges shown. It says nothing about the depth of any channel: shoaling, dredging and survey age matter as much as the water level. Check the live gauge and current surveys, know your own draft and margin, and make your own decision. Use of this page is at your own risk.

"Above chart datum" subtracts an estimated 0.45 ft from the gauge to approximate mean lower low water. That offset has not been surveyed.
